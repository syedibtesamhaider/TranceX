Transcription uploads the captured audio — other people's speech from your call — to ElevenLabs, one request per sentence. Set the ELEVENLABS_API_KEY environment variable; the key is never stored in config.json.

Sent to ElevenLabs as keyterms, which makes these spellings far likelier than acoustic lookalikes. The single most effective fix for domain-word errors. Comma-separated; keep it under a hundred terms.

Latency tuning

A segment that does not end in . ? ! waits for the merge window in case the speaker was only pausing. If a continuation arrives, the combined audio is re-transcribed — not the two texts glued together — so Whisper sees the whole sentence. Questions are only detected on the merged result.

End-of-speech ms is what you actually feel — it is the wait before a finished sentence is sent to the recognizer. The two VAD values only trim silence inside that chunk and barely move latency. Lower values mean faster text but more fragmented sentences.
